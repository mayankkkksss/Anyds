# Anyds — Product Brief

## Status

Planning only. Do not create or modify application source, components, styles, routes, or build configuration until this brief and the companion design plan are explicitly approved.

## Product proposition

Anyds is a single place to discover exceptional web design and motion work. It treats visual design and animation as one experience rather than separating them into different products or sections.

The product should make it easy for designers and developers to browse references, understand what makes a piece compelling, and follow the original creator or library when the work lives elsewhere.

## Confirmed requirements

- Design and animation work together in one discovery system.
- A reference from an external source such as GSAP opens in the same Anyds detail layout as every other reference.
- External references do not expose source code in Anyds.
- Every external reference can show a usable prompt or descriptive brief, clear author attribution, source/library attribution, and a prominent redirect to the original work.
- Anyds should have its own identity; GSAP is an inspiration/source category, not the visual identity.
- No Studio/Admin surface is in scope for the first release.
- Apple Liquid Glass is an influence only: use it selectively for high-value, tactile moments, never as an all-screen coating.

## Primary audiences

1. Designers seeking current, high-quality UI and motion reference.
2. Front-end developers looking for interaction inspiration and a path back to the original library or creator.
3. Creative technologists who want a curated, credited discovery space rather than an unstructured gallery.

## Core job to be done

When I need credible visual or motion inspiration, help me find, understand, and save an example quickly, while making the original creator and source easy to credit and visit.

## MVP boundaries

In scope: browse, filter, search, preview, detail pages, prompt/brief display, author and source credit, outbound redirect, collections/saves (subject to technical review), and editorial curation.

Out of scope: source-code hosting, an admin/studio interface, copying external work, and Liquid Glass applied as a universal background or card treatment.

## Success signals

- A visitor reaches a relevant reference in a few deliberate actions.
- Attribution and the original source are unmistakable.
- Motion adds understanding and delight without obscuring content or harming reduced-motion users.
- The visual system feels authored and calm, not like a generic gallery or a GSAP clone.

## Assumptions requiring approval

- Initial launch is web-first, responsive, and editorially curated.
- Saved collections are private to the visitor in a later MVP increment if authentication is not yet chosen.
- Content metadata includes title, creator, original URL, source/library, tags, short rationale, motion notes, and prompt/brief where permitted.
