About the archive

Provenance is the product

Anyds exists to show exceptional interface motion and name where it came from — creator, source, license and verification status travel with every entry.

Mission

A single place to discover visual design and animation as one experience. Each reference opens in the same detail layout: what moves, how it is choreographed, who made it, and a prominent link back to the original work.

Attribution & removal

Every entry names its creator, role, originating platform and license. Original works are linked out — Anyds never hosts external source code, and recreation briefs are descriptive prompts only.

Attribution shown for entries not yet verified against their origin is labeled accordingly. Creators or rights holders can request corrections or removal and the entry is taken down pending review.

All entries currently in the archive are illustrative sample data — creators, origins and motion values are curated placeholders for interface preview, not verified real-world attributions.

What Anyds does not do

No accounts, no social layer, no trackers. Saved references are stored in your browser only. No code hosting, no claim of conformance against an accessibility standard until a full audit has been completed, and no rebranding of anyone else's work as our own.

Privacy

One localStorage key holds your saved references. It never leaves the device. Zero analytics, zero third-party cookies.
