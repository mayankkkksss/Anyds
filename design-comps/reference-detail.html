Kinetic Spring Topology

Pointer-drag velocity carried into an undamped release, settled by a critically damped spring (ratio 1.0) so the surface stops without bounce.

Bastien Allard · Creative Developer · GSAP Community

Visit Original Source ← Back to Archive

Choreography Rationale

Velocity handoff preserves pointer momentum into an undamped release before settling via critically damped spring dynamics (ratio 1.0) with zero overshoot.

The restraint is the point: 1200ms ceiling, exponential-out easing, and damping ratio exactly 1.0 mean the element never travels further than the gesture implied. Sub-critical ratios would read as toy-like overshoot; super-critical would read as sluggish.

Recreation Brief

Map pointer gesture deltas into instantaneous velocity vectors. On pointerup, feed current velocity into a spring solver configured with stiffness 180 and damping ratio 1.0 to converge smoothly without bounce.
